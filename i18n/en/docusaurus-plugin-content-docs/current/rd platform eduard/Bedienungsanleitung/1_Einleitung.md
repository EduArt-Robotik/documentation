## Symbol Explanation

Pay attention to the warnings and symbols described below to understand potential hazards to the user and device, and thus prevent accidents.

:::danger
Indicates potentially hazardous situations that can result in **death or serious injury** if appropriate safety measures are not taken.
:::

:::warning

:::

:::info

## Intended Use
The training platform **Eduard Performance V3** from **EduArt Robotik GmbH** is designed for education and training within the usage limits (Chapter 2.1) of adolescents and adults of any age. It enables learning from basic knowledge to advanced expertise and research in the fields of **mobile robotics**, **sensors**, and **actuators**.

To serve this purpose, the mobile robot platform offers nearly unlimited expandability in mechanics, electronics, and software.
